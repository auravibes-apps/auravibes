## Evidence types

- `P`: rendered fixture using production `$appRoutes`, with the relevant route/action exercised.
- `B`: rendered production `GoRouteData.build` or `onExit` under a smaller custom GoRouter. Production shell structure and direct URL behavior still need separate checks.
- `W`: rendered actual screen or widget in a standalone, imperative, or custom-route fixture. The route destination may be a placeholder.
- `R`: executed route matching/location/classifier/provider/usecase checks. These do not establish a rendered screen.
- `C`: source-only candidate. Its presence or inspected body does not establish execution.
- `X`: prerequisite that needs a native platform, real email/backend capability, or participant.
