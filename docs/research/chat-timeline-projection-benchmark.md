## Results

Values are microseconds. Each cell is the median of the five per-process sample medians; p95 is the median of the five per-process p95 values.

| Messages | Stable median | Stable p95 | Streaming-tail median | Streaming-tail p95 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | 267 | 810 | 110 | 149 |
| 1,000 | 327 | 604 | 255 | 485 |
| 5,000 | 1,394 | 1,530 | 1,320 | 1,476 |

At 5,000 messages, the isolated projection takes about 1.3–1.4 ms at the median and about 1.5 ms at p95. This is not a material isolated cost against a 16.7 ms frame budget, so the data does not justify speculative projection caching. The small-transcript p95 values show occasional sample outliers, but the 1,000- and 5,000-message cases remain below 1.6 ms.

## Interpretation limits

This measures `ChatTimelineProjection.buildChatTimelineItems` over deterministic synthetic transcripts. It does not measure the full widget rebuild, layout, painting, frame scheduling, or a device’s real streamed response path. Therefore it cannot establish total timeline frame cost or rule out costs elsewhere in the widget path. It also does not measure model-request assembly, hydration, or resume. No cache was added based on this isolated measurement.
